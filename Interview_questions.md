What is the difference b/t inner join and outer join?

If we are using inner join it will give all matching records from both tables and In outer join there are three different joins they are Left outer join, Right outer join, Full outer join. If we are using Left join it will give all the records from left table and matching records from the right table. The same way rightjoin will give all records from right table and matching records from left table. Where as full outer join give all the records from the both tables.
